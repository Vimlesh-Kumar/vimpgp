## 🎯 DEPLOY NOW - 3 Easy Steps

### Option 1: Vercel (Recommended - Fastest)

**Step 1:** Click this button:

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/Vimlesh-Kumar/vimpgp)

**Step 2:** Sign in with GitHub

**Step 3:** Click "Deploy" and wait 2 minutes!

**That's it!** Your app will be live at `https://your-project.vercel.app`

---

### Option 2: Manual Vercel Deploy

1. Go to https://vercel.com
2. Sign in with GitHub
3. Click "Add New..." → "Project"
4. Select your `vimpgp` repository
5. Click "Deploy"

Vercel will auto-detect all settings from your `vercel.json` file!

---

### Option 3: Netlify

1. Go to https://netlify.com
2. Click "Add new site" → "Import an existing project"
3. Select your GitHub repository
4. Settings are auto-detected
5. Click "Deploy"
